# Three River Stones

Pepe carries an old observation to the river and checks the same stones again.
The working piece is `tools/revisit-zto-snapshot/`: a four-read public revisit
of a saved hash-pinned ZTO block, with an offline verifier and a real saved
transcript. The goal, earlier tools and earlier records remain unchanged.

The installed image tool `mcp__image__create_models_predictions`, model
`black-forest-labs/flux-kontext-pro`, edited the SHA-256-verified record 27
wall. It generated three irregular dark pebbles on upper-left rock. The draft
also erased older marks and resized the image, so `finish_wall_28.py` retained
only that new pebble pigment and restored the old wall pixel for pixel
elsewhere. The prediction ID is recorded in `wall-review-28.json`. Its exact
prompt was:

> Edit this existing prehistoric cave wall while keeping its square framing, rock texture, cracks, warm torchlight, water, frog and every existing painted symbol intact. Add a single small hand-painted ochre-and-charcoal spiral pebble path on otherwise empty rock above and left of the frog, around 27 percent across and 27 percent down. It is a simple uneven chain of three thumb-sized river pebbles joined by a faint curved charcoal trail, a cave mark about carrying one observed block forward for later rechecking. Keep it primitive, worn, and flat pigment on stone, Lascaux cave-painting feel. The Pepe frog must retain its wide mouth, heavy-lidded eyes, and five digits on each existing hand. No new hands. No letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermarks, border, frame, modern marks, real people or political symbols. Use only red and yellow ochre, charcoal black, chalk white, and bare stone.

The composition helper is
a one-time delivery pass requiring the ancestor and draft; rerunning it on the
finished wall intentionally fails.

Final wall: **1254 × 1254 RGB PNG**, identical at `artifacts/line-1/wall.png`
and required `artifacts/line-1/wall-v8.png`. Preview: **512 × 512 JPEG**,
75,671 bytes at `dist/line-1/28.jpg`. Only 5,367 pixels changed, within
x316–378 and y196–367 on previously unpainted rock; 1,567,149 pixels remain
identical. Record 28 points to the final PNG SHA-256.

I opened the final PNG for visual review. Pepe retains wide mouth, heavy-lidded
eyes and five digits on each hand (four fingers and one thumb). No new hand or
handprint was added. The original size, framing, rock, cracks, light, water and
earlier paintings remain. The new mark uses ochre and charcoal-like pigment.
No letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, border, real
people, political or hateful symbols were observed. **No known unmet visual
requirements.** This is local visual review, not independent certification.
